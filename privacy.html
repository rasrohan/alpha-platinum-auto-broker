Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Alpha Platinum Auto Broker collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website or ask us to help source a vehicle.

Effective October 16, 2026 · Last updated October 16, 2026

Who we are

Alpha Platinum Auto Broker is a DBA of Rowtronic LLC. We provide buyer intake, vehicle sourcing support, procurement coordination, dealer communication, and logistics guidance. Contact us at rowtronicconsulting@gmail.com or +1 (678) 387-0355.

Information we collect

Information you choose to provide may include your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, destination, address or port details, vehicle preferences, budget, timeline, buyer eligibility confirmations, questions, and consent choices. If you continue to Stripe, Stripe collects payment information under its own privacy terms; this website and Maria do not collect card or bank credentials.

How we use information

  • Prepare, review, and respond to a vehicle sourcing request.
  • Estimate the appropriate concierge service lane and coordinate follow-up.
  • Communicate with licensed dealers, inspection, logistics, payment, and other service providers when needed for the request.
  • Protect the business, comply with law, and perform eligibility or sanctions-related review where applicable.
  • Maintain service, security, and transaction records.

Website and handoff boundaries

The current website is a static intake and checkout-routing experience. It does not promise that a request was automatically delivered merely because a form was completed. If you choose Gmail, your email app, WhatsApp, Stripe, or another third-party service, that provider processes information under its own terms. Review the destination and message before sending.

Sharing

We may share information with Rowtronic personnel and service providers that support a request, including licensed selling dealers, inspectors, logistics providers, payment processors, professional advisers, and authorities when legally required. We do not sell personal information for advertising.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no email, messaging platform, payment service, or internet transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure. Do not send card numbers, bank credentials, passwords, or government-ID images through Maria or an ordinary email unless the team gives you an approved secure method.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information you provided, or withdraw future contact consent, by emailing us. Some information may be retained when required for legal, security, accounting, or transaction-record purposes. Browser settings can control cookies used by third-party services you choose to visit.

International visitors and minors

Information may be processed in the United States. The service is intended for adults age 18 or older. Destination selection does not guarantee export or import eligibility.

Updates and contact

We may update this notice as the service changes. The date above identifies the latest version. Privacy questions or requests may be sent to rowtronicconsulting@gmail.com or mailed to Rowtronic LLC, 909 Eagles Landing Pkwy Ste 440 #2159, Stockbridge, GA 30281.

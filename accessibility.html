Accessibility

Alpha Platinum Auto Broker wants buyers and partners to be able to understand our services and complete a request using the website or an assisted channel.

Last updated October 16, 2026

Our current approach

We are working toward a practical WCAG 2.1 Level AA experience. The website includes semantic headings, labeled form controls, keyboard-visible focus, a skip link, reduced-motion support, descriptive status messages, responsive layouts, and text alternatives for meaningful images.

Alternative assistance

If any part of the website is difficult to use, the team can help review a vehicle request by email or phone. Contact rowtronicconsulting@gmail.com, +1 (678) 387-0355, or Operations Manager Lance Johnson at +1 (518) 698-7516.

Report a barrier

Please tell us the page or feature involved, the device and browser used, and the assistance you need. Do not include card numbers, bank credentials, passwords, or sensitive identity documents in an accessibility email.

Third-party services

Stripe, Gmail, WhatsApp, email applications, and other linked services maintain their own accessibility features. We welcome reports about handoff barriers so we can offer another reasonable route where available.
